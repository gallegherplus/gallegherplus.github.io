How to Use

1. Connect iRig

Plug your iRig audio interface into your phone. Tap 🎤 Input to enable it.

2. Select Effect

Choose an effect from the tabs above the pad (Delay, Filter, Reverb, Drive, Flanger, Phaser).

3. Touch the Pad

Drag your finger on the XY pad to control the effect. X-axis = parameter 1, Y-axis = parameter 2.

4. Bypass Toggle

Tap Bypass to toggle effects on/off. Green LED = active, Off = bypassed.
